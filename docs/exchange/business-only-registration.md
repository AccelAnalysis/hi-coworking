# Business-only registration

Registration creates a person account for an authorized representative; it does not create or verify organization authority.

Version 2 requires full name, professional title, private email, private telephone, essential communication preferences, and the attestation: “I am registering as an authorized representative of a business or organization.”

The login/private email is not automatically an organization contact. Public professional contact remains separately controlled. Organization authority still requires an exact-active membership created through canonical organization creation or an approved governed claim.

There is no individual-only registration, browse-as-individual action, skip-organization action, or completed activation without an organization. A claim-pending user is labeled “Organization claim pending” and receives no actor authority or private actor location.
