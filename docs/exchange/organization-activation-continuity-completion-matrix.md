## Overall assessment

The code-side organization activation and continuity workstream is approximately
72% complete when weighted across the Phase 22 rows. This is not a release
percentage. The largest implemented gains are explicit actor/subject contracts,
final viewer-relative projection allowlists, exact-active authority, a single
persistent map, per-mode state, URL/session restoration, and guarded seed
tooling.

The honest configured-development completion is materially lower because the
new Functions, rules, indexes, Storage, and Hosting have not yet been accepted;
the browser matrix has not run; authoritative locality evidence is pending; and
zero seed rows have human approval. The workstream must not be reported as 100%
until those gates pass. The broader Exchange cannot be reported as 100% even
then because the separate Issuer RFx, Responder drafting, Evaluation, Teaming,
complete Resources, live commerce, transactional communications, and production
release workstreams remain.
