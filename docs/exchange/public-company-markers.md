# Public company markers

A public marker requires:

- active organization and approved directory publication;
- active physical establishment with entered address;
- governed confirmed geocode;
- non-home, non-virtual, non-mailing classification; and
- explicit coordinate-publication approval.

Address publication remains independent. A directory-approved organization with no permitted coordinate can remain searchable/list-only. The settings checklist exposes organization approval, address, confirmation, coordinate approval, and resulting public marker state.

No opportunity record participates in this gate.
