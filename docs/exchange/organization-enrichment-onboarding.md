# Organization enrichment onboarding

Enrichment is the first organization-settings step, before manual UEI, CAGE, DUNS, NAICS, certification, capability, media, or document entry.

Proposed fields, addresses, coordinates, telephone numbers, and emails remain proposals until individually classified. Acceptance writes organization-owned records and preserves provenance. It never grants a claim, establishes verification, creates a headquarters silently, replaces an owner-confirmed establishment, or approves directory/address/coordinate/contact publication.

Completing the review records `activationEnrichmentReviewedAt` under exact owner/admin authority. Organization Profile follows the review.
