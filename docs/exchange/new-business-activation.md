# New business activation

The guided order is:

1. Representative essentials and attestation.
2. Organization search, governed claim, or creation.
3. Enrichment review.
4. Organization profile and directory-publication decision.
5. Primary/headquarters establishment, address search, coordinate confirmation, and independent publication choices.
6. Orientation-establishment choice.
7. Private operational contact plus referral and opportunity routes.
8. Close 3D Exchange activation.

`exchange_getBusinessActivationState` supplies the current step, completed and blocked steps, allowed actions, organization/claim/authority identifiers, establishment precedence, private/public marker states, and safe resume route. Browser storage is not canonical completion state.
