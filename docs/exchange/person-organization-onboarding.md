# Person and organization onboarding

Person records own the representative’s name, title, private contact preferences, accessibility/notification preferences, and preferred orientation establishment.

Organization records own legal/trade names, domain, website, description, industries, capabilities, procurement identifiers, certifications, media, documents, publication status, establishments, contact points, and communication routes.

Legacy person-profile business fields remain compatibility data only. They do not silently overwrite organization-owned records. Registration enters organization onboarding directly instead of the complete legacy profile editor.

Refresh and sign-in resume from server-derived activation state. Actor URL parameters remain requests and never prove authority.
